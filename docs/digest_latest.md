## Electricity & affordability

  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in House

  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in House

  Passed one chamber. Latest (2026-09-30): Committee on Energy and Natural Resources. Ordered to be reported without amendment favorably.  
  *Why tracked:* The bill addresses grid reliability and resilience through state energy security plans covering distribution systems, bulk-power system, and equipment supply chain risks. It does not address rates or costs directly.
- **[S 4166](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/4166): SECURE Grid Act**

## Data centers & communities

**4 changed bills, ranked by relevance.**

- **[HR 10950](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/10950): Data Center Oversight Act**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-10-29): Introduced in House  
  *Why tracked:* Requires public disclosure by large data center sponsors covering siting near protected areas, water use, generation, ownership, and nondisclosure agreements with governments.
- **[S 5640](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5640): Data Center Transparency Act of 2026**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-30): Introduced in Senate  
  *Why tracked:* The main subject is reporting on the water, air, greenhouse gas, and electricity effects of large data centers on local water systems and overburdened communities.
- **[S 5645](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5645): Data Center Community Empowerment Act of 2026**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-30): Introduced in Senate  
  *Why tracked:* The bill's main subject is helping State, local, and Tribal governments assess the energy, water, economic, and community impacts of data center development.
- **[HR 9340](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/9340): Ratepayer Protection Act**  
  Passed one chamber. Latest (2026-09-30): Senate roll call 254  
  *Why tracked:* Defines large-load customers as data-center-type facilities of 100 MW or more and sets the financial terms for serving them, which bears on the community cost effects of computing infrastructure.

## Jobs & skills

**7 changed bills, ranked by relevance.**

- **[HRES 1607](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-resolution/1607): Expressing support for the designation of September 2026 as "National Workforce Development Month".**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-10-05): Introduced in House  
  *Why tracked:* The resolution's main subject is workforce development, covering skills training, WIOA, apprenticeships, and job disruption.
- **[HR 10561](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/10561): Workforce Opportunities for Communities in Recovery Act**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in House  
  *Why tracked:* A broad WIOA workforce grant pilot for dislocated, incumbent, and new workers affected by substance use disorder, with training and career services. It has no automation link but is a broad workforce program.
- **[HR 10578](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/10578): AI DATA Act**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in House  
  *Why tracked:* The bill's main subject is measuring AI and automation effects on hiring, separations, occupational demand, skills, and retraining through new federal surveys and an annual report.
- **[S 5518](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5518): Make AI Work for Americans Act**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in Senate  
  *Why tracked:* Main subject: a fund to train workers, mitigate AI-driven job loss, and expand unemployment insurance, workforce training, and AI workplace rules.
- **[S 5524](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5524): Workforce Opportunities for Communities in Recovery Act**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-24): Introduced in Senate  
  *Why tracked:* Main subject is a workforce grant program under WIOA providing job training and reemployment for workers affected by substance use disorder, a broad workforce program.
- **[HRES 1642](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-resolution/1642): Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that Congress must refuse to provide any additional funds or exceptional regulatory support to companies or financial institutions facing insolvency or financial collapse due to exposures to acute stress within the artificial intelligence market and must provide support to all harmed Americans instead.**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-10-29): Introduced in House  
  *Why tracked:* Operative clauses call for job creation, unemployment insurance, and worker protections if an AI bubble collapse causes job losses. This is not about skills or task change.
- **[SRES 918](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-resolution/918): A resolution establishing the Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence.**  
  Introduced. Latest (2026-09-29): Introduced in Senate  
  *Why tracked:* One of the committee's enumerated mandates is examining the labor effects of AI, including displacement and workforce development.
